Features

One click shows where you stand.

Both limits at a glance

The menu bar icon shows your 5-hour and 7-day usage. Click it for exact numbers and reset times.

Per-model usage

Weekly usage for each model your plan limits separately, each with its own reset time.

History up to 30 days

See how fast you're using your limits and when each window reset. Hover for exact values.

Extra usage in dollars

If your plan allows extra usage, see what you've spent against your monthly limit.

Refresh on your schedule

Every 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes or hour, and it can start when you log in.

Updates itself

It checks for a new version once a day and asks before installing. You can turn that off.

Privacy

Free and open source.
Your data stays on your Mac.

Tokens and usage history are stored on your Mac. The app only talks to Anthropic and GitHub.

  • Stored files are readable only by you
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Updates are signed and verified before they install
